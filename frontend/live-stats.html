Turnaround Target Benchmarks (Service Level Agreement)

Campus Fix enforces dynamic deadline thresholds based on report criticality. Tickets exceeding these limits are flagged for administrative escalation.

Critical Urgency
≤ 24 Hours

Flooding, major electrical failure, lab fume hood risk, or fire hazard.

High Urgency
≤ 48 Hours

Lecture hall projector down, Wi-Fi outage, classroom AC tripping.

Medium Urgency
≤ 72 Hours

Single light tube flickering, slow restroom drain, loose door handle.

Low Urgency
≤ 120 Hours

Minor cosmetic marks, desk chair cylinder tune-up, routine checks.
