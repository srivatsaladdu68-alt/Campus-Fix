How Campus Fix Works

A transparent 3-step cycle ensuring zero maintenance requests are lost.

1

Report The Fault

Students or faculty select the facility, specify the building and room, snap a photo, and submit the ticket with urgency details.

2

Verify & Assign

Administrators verify complaint urgency and instantly dispatch the appropriate technician (Electrician, Plumber, IT) with clear deadlines.

3

Fix & Confirm

Staff execute repairs, record notes, and mark tasks resolved. Reporters receive instant updates and can reopen if issues persist.
