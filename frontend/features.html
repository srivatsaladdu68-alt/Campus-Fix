Complete System Architecture & Capabilities

Engineered for Fast, Accountable Campus Fixes

Discover how Campus Fix replaces disorganized manual reporting with intelligent detection, transparent tracking, automated staff dispatch, and data-driven university facilities maintenance.

Core Capabilities

End-to-End Maintenance Intelligence

Designed to address the exact breakdown points in university campus facility management.

Rapid Fault Reporting & Photo Upload

Students and faculty can report faults in under a minute with building, floor, room number, urgency levels (Low, Medium, High, Critical), and optional photo upload with client-side preview.

Smart Duplicate Detection

Prevents duplicate work tickets by checking the database in real-time. If a problem is already open in that exact room and category, reporters are alerted before submitting.

Visual Status Progress Timeline

Track complaints step-by-step from Reported → Verified → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved → Closed, complete with full audit timestamps and technician notes.

Automated Overdue Detection

Critical tickets set a 24-hour turnaround target, while High priority sets 48 hours. If unresolved when the deadline expires, the system flags the ticket with red pulsing overdue alerts.

User Reopen Mechanism

If an issue persists after being marked resolved, students and faculty can reopen the ticket with a description, automatically sending a high-priority dispatch notice back to facilities.

In-App Notification Center

Near real-time alerts notify reporters when complaints are verified, assigned, started, completed, or overdue, with unread counters and 1-click mark-all-read.

Governance & Analytics

Administrative Power & Performance Oversight

Equipping campus facility directors with real-time diagnostics and staff management tools.

Real-Time Chart.js Visualizations

Interactive analytics tracking 14-day complaint trends, category breakdown doughnut charts, building failure distributions, and staff workload balances based entirely on real database data.

Recurring Problem Spot Alerts

Intelligent maintenance automatically pinpoints rooms and labs with recurring failures (≥ 2 complaints), helping administrators replace aging equipment before final failure.

One-Click CSV Reports Export

Filter work orders by date range, facility category, urgency, and status, and export RFC-4180 compliant CSV spreadsheets for university inspections and compliance records.

Work Order Dispatch & Resource Flagging

Assign qualified electricians or plumbers with custom deadlines. Technicians can log inspection notes and flag tickets when spare parts or budget approval is needed.

Future IoT Telemetry Ingestion

Designed with open REST APIs (/api/iot/events) to receive automated readings from water leak sensors, room temperature probes, and power meters with automated ticket creation.

Enterprise-Grade Security

Parameterized SQLite queries prevent SQL injection, Bcrypt hashes all passwords, and JWT middleware enforces strict role-based access control across all operations.
